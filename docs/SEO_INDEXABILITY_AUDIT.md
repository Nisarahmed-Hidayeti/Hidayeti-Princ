# SEO indexability audit

Baseline on 13 November 2026. The static HTML, CSS and JavaScript site deploys from GitHub main through Netlify. The existing Node generator creates product pages, the wholesale page, metadata, and the sitemap without dependencies. Production base commit was `c6eee9d`.

- All eight sitemap URLs returned 200 over HTTPS with self-canonicals, initial HTML content, unique titles and descriptions, and crawlable links. Robots permits crawling and references the sitemap. Explicit index URLs redirect to canonical directories. The editor has noindex and unknown paths return 404.
- The sitemap has eight distinct canonical URLs. It omits lastmod because the catalogue lacks a trustworthy content date. This avoids publishing artificial dates.
- The homepage stylesheet warning is a fallback element. In the production browser it was hidden, the header had its expected sticky styling, and all five catalogue cards rendered. The fallback required no repair.
- Site metadata uses Hidayeti Pirinç. Product names and pack artwork retain the catalogue’s Hidayeti Prinç and Buharı Prinç spellings. Existing Organization alternateName covers the legacy spelling.
- Search Console showed sitemap **Success**, last read 13 November 2026, and **eight discovered pages**. Homepage indexing and HTTPS were confirmed previously by the owner. URL Inspection reported all five products and wholesale outside the index. The wholesale and Hidayeti 5 kg URLs were unknown to Google in their individual reports. The other four product URLs were discovered and awaiting indexing. Individual URL reports can lag the sitemap report. [Search Console sitemap report](https://search.google.com/search-console/sitemaps?resource_id=sc-domain%3Ahidayetipirinc.com)
- Product, Organization, WebSite and BreadcrumbList JSON-LD were parsed and checked in the deployed HTML. Breadcrumbs meet the checked structure of named sequential links. Product is descriptive only and lacks the offers, reviews or ratings needed for Google product snippets. Direct purchase and the required offer data are absent for merchant listings. [Google product structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product) · [Google product snippets](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product-snippet)
