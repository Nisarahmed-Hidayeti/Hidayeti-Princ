# Google growth plan

Public Turkish supplier results show Basmati pages organized by pack size and wholesale catalogues. This provides evidence of how suppliers answer these queries. Demand, ranking and conversion remain hypotheses until Search Console and actual inquiry records provide data. [Albak catalogue](https://albakgida.com/) · [Kings Crown pack sizes](https://kingscrown.com.tr/tr/urun/uzun-taneli-sella-basmati-pirinci-buharda-pisirilmis/)

| Priority | Search intent | Destination | Next content improvement |
|---|---|---|---|
| High | Hidayeti Pirinç, Hidayeti Basmati | Homepage and Hidayeti pack pages | Track brand spelling variants. Add verified packaging facts. |
| High | Buharı Basmati, 40 kg basmati pirinç | Buharı pages, especially 40 kg | Link buyers to the quote option for the exact pack. |
| High | 5 kg basmati pirinç, 10 kg basmati pirinç | Corresponding pack pages | Explain verified differences between families when available. |
| High | Toptan basmati pirinç, toptan pirinç teklifi, restoranlar için basmati pirinç | Wholesale page | Use pack, quantity, and delivery province in quote drafts. Add confirmed minimums and delivery details. |
| Medium | Basmati pirinç, toptan pirinç, pirinç tedarikçisi | Homepage or wholesale, depending on intent | Use query data to separate broader browsing from quote intent. |

**30 days.** Track Search Console discovery and indexing for the six priority URLs. Keep a private weekly count of incoming inquiries, qualified wholesale requests and confirmed orders by pack.

**60 days.** Use actual questions to draft a short pack-size guide and add approved answers to the wholesale page. A pack guide can explain the existing options and how to calculate the number of packs for a known quantity.

**90 days.** Compare impressions, clicks and CTR across 28-day periods for brand, pack and wholesale groups. Compare qualified inquiries alongside traffic. Prioritize a comparison page only after product differences are verified. Check field LCP, INP and CLS when data is sufficient.

## Google sales features

Merchant Center online listings are currently ineligible. This is a WhatsApp inquiry catalogue without fixed public prices, availability data, direct secure checkout, payment options, or published shipping and return terms. Reassess only if the business implements these features and can keep the product data current. [Google Merchant Center requirements](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/12160471)

Google Business Profile eligibility depends on in-person customer contact during stated hours. The available site information does not establish this. An online-only operation generally does not qualify. [Google Business Profile eligibility](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036)
